One key to dictate with Handy on macOS: with Handy installed, turn on "Start Handy recording when the dictation box opens" under Settings › Menu bar, and ⌃⌥Space opens the dictation box with Handy already listening. Stop Handy with its own key to read the text before sending, or press Enter while it records to stop it and send the transcription as soon as it arrives. Esc, or anything else that closes the box, cancels Handy. Off by default. Updating restarts the daemon (contract 40).
