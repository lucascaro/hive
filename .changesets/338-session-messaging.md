Message a session. ⇧⌘M hands the active session a note — typed into its terminal when it is quiet, or queued for its next idle moment if it is working (an ACP session takes it as a turn). ⌥⌘B, or the bell on a session's row, notifies you once when the session stops working. `hived msg <session> <text…>` does the same from a shell.
