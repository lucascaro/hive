- Settings is now split into three tabs — Agents, Appearance and Updates —
  instead of one long scroll. It opens on Agents, so the custom-agent list is
  the first thing you see however many agents you have, and the theme picker
  and update channel are one click away rather than a scroll away. Switching
  tabs never discards an edit in progress, and the tabs are keyboard-navigable
  with the arrow keys.
