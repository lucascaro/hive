Two new theme presets in Settings → Appearance → Theme, under Community:
**Alucard** and **Hex**. Alucard is a warm-black and antique-gold palette
sampled off Ayami Kojima's *Symphony of the Night* artwork — cream type, gold
accent, moonlight-blue for informational state, and red kept for errors alone.
Hex is the dark-purple one: ink-violet grounds with an amethyst accent, the
saturation held in the chrome so body text stays near-neutral. Neither is a
port of another editor's theme, so unlike the twelve palettes beside them both
are held to the full WCAG contrast gate rather than opting out of it.
