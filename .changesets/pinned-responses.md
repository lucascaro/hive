Pinned responses: Claude and Pi sessions (except ACP Pi) can pin an important answer — a decision, a root cause, a recipe — with a title, and Hive keeps it in a Pinned section of the inspector (⌘J), newest first, collapsible so the activity view stays usable. Copy a pin, unpin it, or unpin all after a confirm; the sidebar row shows each session's pin count, activity-grid tiles list the titles, and pins survive restarts, reopen and unarchive. Agents pin on their own initiative by default; turn it off under Settings → Agents.
